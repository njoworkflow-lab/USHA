## Results

- 194 source/content assertions and 3 existing brief-form tests passed.
- Two builds with Node.js 22.23.4 produce identical results across 74 generated files.
- Chromium 154.0.8037.57: 779 browser assertions over 59 page/viewport combinations; no console errors, page errors or failed asset requests.
- All 29 sitemap pages have the expected language, canonical link, heading and structured data; all pages and four crawler files return 200.
- All changed pages pass at 360, 390 and 1440 px. Arabic remains right-to-left.
- `USHA_LIVE` is enabled on all 29 pages; live chat submissions were not made.

**Inherited exception:** Russian `/premiere/` has horizontal overflow at 360 px (document width 561 px; horizontal scroll 201 px). Its HTML is byte-identical to the starting preview. It remains unchanged under the explicit instruction to leave PREMIERE alone. The other 28 sitemap pages pass at 360 px.

Supplemental interaction checks passed: home after entry at 360 px in all three languages, six normal-motion rapid-switch scenarios, and RU → EN → RU hero text/destination switching.
