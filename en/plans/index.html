Questions

Before you decide.

Who approves the moves, and who answers for them?

You approve anything that spends money, sets a price or speaks for your brand for the first time. She answers for the decision and its arithmetic. Your project manager, named on day one, answers for delivery. Every yes is logged: who approved what, and when.

What if a move doesn’t work?

It stops at its stop rule, and she brings you plan B. If the work itself falls short, she re-checks it the same day, a senior expert fixes it within one working day, and then the head of strategy steps in. If a commitment in your order form is still missed, you get money back on the order form’s terms. The commitments are guaranteed; results the market decides are not.

How does pricing work? Are ad budgets included?

Each plan is a fixed monthly fee, set in an order form with your project manager after the free week. Prices are agreed to your scope, and none are published. Ad budgets, creator fees and collaboration products are paid separately, and only on your approval.

Will she replace my agency or my team?

Only if you want her to. Most owners keep both and give them her direction: what to make, where to spend, what to stop. She works alongside your agency, and your team gets decisions instead of guesses.

What happens to my data, and can I leave?

An NDA is signed before you share any data, and access stays in your name. You can export your work as PDF and CSV at any time. On a written request, your data is deleted within 30 days. A paid plan stops with 7 days’ notice.

How much of my time does it take?

In the month you clicked through above, 62 minutes. She asks you for at most three decisions a day, each with its arithmetic, and you answer Approve or Not now. There’s no new system to learn.

Isn’t this the same AI everyone has?

Everyone has the same models. She was trained on what others don’t have: the methods, decisions and market knowledge behind 700+ projects in 20+ countries. Every move passes a check before it reaches you: numbers recalculated, sources dated within 30 days, brand style matched, claims backed, the right language.

Which markets and sectors?

The Gulf (Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman), Egypt and Russia, in English, Arabic and Russian. Any sector that sells: retail, consumer goods, beauty, fashion, food, real estate, automotive, health, services and B2B. The full platform list is in Plans.
