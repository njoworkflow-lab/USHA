Seven hires or one engine

Covering the same work in-house takes at least seven people: a head of marketing, a marketer, a paid media specialist, a social media manager, a video editor, an AI creator and a designer. That is months of hiring, training and managing, plus holidays and sick leave. USHA starts in a week.

When each one fits

  • AI tools suit one-off tasks when you know exactly what to ask and are ready to check the facts.
  • An agency suits large campaigns with a separate budget, but works through a manager and on request.
  • An in-house team knows the business from the inside, until people leave, and depends on hiring.
  • USHA fits when the market needs reading every day and the work needs doing straight away: research, publishing and measuring.
