Marketplace analytics that ends in a move

A sales report shows what already happened. On a marketplace, four algorithms decide your sales, and their rules change every month. Analytics is only useful when it says what changed, why, and what to do today.

Four algorithms decide your sales

On a marketplace you do not sell to the buyer directly. You sell through four systems:

  • Search decides whether your card is on page 1 or page 9.
  • The feed and recommendations decide who sees the product at all.
  • The ad auction decides what each click and each customer costs.
  • The buyer decides in seven seconds whether to trust the card.

Posting more will not beat them. Reading them every day, and answering their changes before competitors do, will.

What to read every day

Every day USHA reads 1,240 sources around a business. For a marketplace seller that means:

  • competitors' prices, promotions and bundles on Wildberries, Ozon and Yandex Market;
  • your cards' positions and which searches are growing;
  • the titles, first images and structure of the leaders' cards;
  • reviews, yours and competitors', with repeated complaints and praise;
  • ads, and the hours when demand really arrives.
