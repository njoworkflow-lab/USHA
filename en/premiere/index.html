    What’s real here

    What’s simulated, what’s real, and why we tell you.

    Simulated

    Simulated crowds (every plan)

    AI viewers, each with a profile: age, city, language, what they watch, how they scroll, whether they’ve seen you before. Modelled on real audience data. Nobody real sees your work, and the same crowd can watch your next cut for a fair comparison.

    Good at: comparing versions, finding the second people leave, showing who it’s not for, catching what could backfire.

    Not: a forecast of your views. Simulated panels follow the direction of real reactions well and tend to overstate how strong they are. So you get rankings and weak spots, never promised numbers.

    Questions creators ask

    Are these real people?

    No, not in the standard crowds, and we’d rather you hear it from us. Every plan’s crowd is simulated: AI viewers, each with a profile (age, city, language, what they watch, how they scroll, whether they know you), modelled on real audience data. That’s why it’s instant, private and repeatable. Real people appear only in the real-person panels (creators, creative directors, brand PR people). Those are an add-on, always labelled “Real people”, and take 3 to 7 working days.

    How can an AI viewer “watch” a video?

    It reads your video the way the feed delivers it: frame by frame, the on-screen text, and what’s said and played, second by second. Then it decides, as that person would, whether to keep watching, and says why in their own words.

    Will it tell me how many views I’ll get?

    No. Views depend on the platform, the day and a lot of luck. PREMIERE tells you which version is stronger, the second people leave, who it’s not for and what could backfire. Those are the things you can still change.

    How accurate is it?

    Good at direction, careful about size. Research on simulated panels finds they follow the direction of real reactions well (which version wins, what people dislike) and tend to overstate how strong those reactions are. So read the report as a ranking, not a forecast. After you post, add your real numbers (“Calibrate”) and your next crowd adjusts to your audience.

    Who sees my unreleased video?

    In a simulated test, no person does. If you order a real-person panel, only that panel sees it, under a confidentiality agreement.

    Does it understand Arabic dialects and Russian?

    Yes. Crowds watch in Gulf, Egyptian or Levantine Arabic, English or Russian, and their comments come back in their own words, with a translation on tap. If your video’s language doesn’t match part of your crowd, the builder tells you, and the test shows what happens to them.

    Why can’t I pay on the site?

    For now, our team activates every plan personally. Reserve your plan, and we message you on WhatsApp or Telegram, answer your questions and explain how to pay. This site never asks for card details.

    What are the real-person panels, and how long do they take?

    Real creators in your niche and size (from 50 followers to millions), real creative directors, and real PR people from brands in your category. Each panel is an add-on on any plan, labelled “Real people”, and replies in 3 to 7 working days. You see their notes, not their names.

    Can it tell me whether a brand will work with me?

    It can tell you how a brand’s PR team is likely to read your content: brand-safe or not, which categories fit, and what they’d ask for first. The simulated Brand PR read comes with Arena; the real one is an add-on. Neither is an offer of a deal, and no brand is contacted.

    Why not just show it to friends?

    Friends are kind, few, and they’ve already met you. Your next viewer is a stranger on a fast scroll. PREMIERE gives you hundreds of strangers who match your audience, tells you the second they leave, and lets the same crowd judge your next cut fairly. Friends can’t unsee version one.
